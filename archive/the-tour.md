# The Tour

> Researchers from 2436 study a locked-down student of 2020, logged as timestamped field notes. He never gets a name — only a scholar number, and the notes stop before the record does.

0903 HOURS: JAN 23, 2436
proff. Bakshi has yet again denied my request to change the allotted year for research to me, for now it seems everyone has got better options. Like 2399-the year MANIT launched its first intergalactic hyper space-drive cum time traveler, 2276-student exchange program with mars, 2132-inauguration of MANIT's student outreach program on the moon. Hell, even manan gets to visit 1960, to witness the foundation of MACT, but i? Huh… I get to visit AD-2020. The great year of the quarantined.

0945 HOURS: JAN 23, 2436
standing beside the time pod which has my sch. no. 4341115202 etched on it, Mr. Sain awaits me with a huge grin. It's his first time too. The PhD scholar was my guide and companion on this trip, who certainly seemed more enthusiastic than me, "be ready to have your mind blown!" I highly doubt that. At the very least, I'm sure I know much more than them. what can their primitive ways of living teach the humans who have evolved beyond their imagination?

0950 HOURS: JAN 23, 2436 :: 0803 HOURS : AUG 14, 2020
switching it on, the machine wobbles and shakes. Faces stretch, stomach churns. events ranging over four centuries race by. Mr. Sain and I hold tight as a boom marks the end of our travel. We have arrived. I walk out nauseas, into an empty park. is this always this empty here? I know population is just a meager 7.9 billion, but still. Mr. Sain and I activate our invisibility suits. Packing the time pod into his backpack, he says, "let's move. We have a long way to go before we reach 191114098's house."

0820 HOURS: AUG 14, 2020
what a strange house. No pads to land ships or jetpacks. Making our way in I ask, "can he hear us?" to which Mr. Sain replies, "No he can't. Remember we shall address 191114098 as 'subject', to maintain confidentiality." and there in front of me was he, sleeping in a mess. "He is short."
"No, no. it's the average height in this timeline."

0902 HOURS: AUG 14, 2020
subject wakes up, switches on his laptop and goes back to sleep. "See carefully, this technique used by the subject is meant to evade the current prevalent, online classes. Studies show that they weren’t much into studying." if they didn’t attend classes, how did they pass? Still the students cleared all the subjects. Interesting.

0956 HOURS: AUG 14, 2020
with his eyes still clenched shut, he moves out of the bed, "Notice how he walks. The gait has been developed to conserve as much energy as he can, which would further be needed in other laborious activities. Like switching on the TV, browsing YouTube, etc."

1016 HOURS; AUG 14, 2020
the subject settles on the couch with a thud. "This is the central hub for all activities, where the quarantined manage all of their work from. They excel in high level multitasking. Which you can see now as the subject handles online classes, Netflix, chatting and eating poha at the same time." how can he do so much with ease? Clearly I underestimated them.

1032 HOURS: AUG 14, 2020
a parent lectures the subject on importance of routines and focusing on online classes. But the subject groans and continues on. "the subject has developed a form of resistance to any outside intrusion."

1246 HOURS: AUG 14, 2020
the subject controls a character in his mobile device. "What's he doing?" Mr. Sain flips his notes to find the exact term, "it's called… PUBG. was a very important part of the lifestyle of the quarantined, it allows one's character to kill other's character in a virtual environment. requires a very intricate skill set. Possessed by very few." what else can they do? They continue to surprise me.

1422 HOURS: AUG 12, 2020
another parent cusses about lack of exercise, forcing the subject to submit this time. Whimpering, he dawdles out. He struggles to step out, retracting immediately realizing the sun's up. "Sunlight scorches their skin and burns their eyes. Staying indoors for months caused this withdrawal."

1502 HOURS: AUG 12, 2020
a ting sound from his mobile device brings dismay. "This daily occurrence is called assignments. A tradition of this time, where the tutor hands some work which has to be submitted in a given time frame." could I have endured this grueling life? {more here}

1511 HOURS: AUG 12, 2020
he lies like a bloated cushion on the couch. his face gets lively as soon as he joins a video chat with his friends, talking loud and lively. visibly happy, this little amount of contact seems to be a necessity. he used plethora of words, which didn’t make much sense to me. like, what does fundae mean?

1854 HOURS: AUG 12, 2020
he finally gets on his feet. Watching a cookery video on his mobile device he tries to replicate what he sees. "Mr. sain, I can't understand how do they cope with this? even after so much restrictions, assignments, he seems fine. Doesn’t the sedentary lifestyle, the strain bother him?"
"every generation has its strong points. maybe tolerance is theirs."

2156 HOURS: AUG 12, 2020
In yet again a commendable display of multitasking, he juggles between dinner, assignments and TV. managing different segments of assignments from his friends into one, he barely makes the deadline. but still he remains cool. their ability to work through all this fascinates me. to just accept and carry on.

0142 HOURS: AUG 13, 2020
Mr. Sain signals that it's time to leave. I take one last look at him, busy being addicted to his mobile device, but Mr. Sain tells me it's what connects him to the world. I wonder why they haven’t figured out mental chips yet.

0203 HOURS: AUG 13, 2020 :: 0950 HOURS: JAN 23, 2436
In an empty alley, Mr. Sain prepares the time pod. The same flashing lights, churning stomach and loud boom constitute our return to the present. Other travelers had made their way back too. Mr. Bakshi hands me a form which titles - 'what have you learnt?' other kids grown, complaining how hectic everything is. Mr. Sain and I smile, for we had witnessed something much more grounded. Being carefree but {?} at the same time. I didn’t wait for a minute and wrote down - the ability to cope.
