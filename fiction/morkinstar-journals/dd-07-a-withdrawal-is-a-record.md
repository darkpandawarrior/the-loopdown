# A Withdrawal Is A Record

> Request 11-0509 · class III · returned complete

---

**REQUEST SLIP**\
Form R/2\
Reading Room counter\
Received: click 140, galaxal 901

Requester: Vunn. Class requested: III.

Description of what is sought, in the requester's hand, reproduced as supplied:

> I filed a request at this counter on click 129 of this galaxal, in my own name, and I would like it withdrawn. I do not want an answer to it. I have not wanted one for a good while now and I would rather it was not sitting somewhere still owed to me.
>
> The form asks what it was about. I am not going to write it down a second time, and ~~that is the~~ not writing it down is the whole of my difficulty, so I am hoping the date and the name will do. I was told at the counter that they will do. Please take it out.
>
> Then when it is out, please do not keep the taking out. I can hear how that sounds. I am not asking anybody to do a job twice. I am asking for it to be done the once, and for no note to be made of it.
>
> It is eleven clicks. I know that without counting, though I did count, because I am the sort who counts. I was in a poor way that year and I have not been in a poor way since. I have a trade, and a roof that is mine, and a woman who puts up with me, and the boy. There is nothing left over from that year that anybody can put a hand on, excepting the one thing, and the one thing is in this building.
>
> I have brought the date written down in case I say it wrong out loud, which I have already done twice today.
>
> I have a boy at home who is nine and who reads everything there is. Jars, tickets, the back of other people's letters, the tin. I asked at the counter whether anybody at all can walk in here and be handed a thing, and the answer was yes, and it was given plainly, and it was the right answer, and I have not slept properly since.
>
> I will pay what it costs, and I know it will not be nothing. I am not making a complaint and there is nothing to complain of. Please.
>
> Vunn. Click 140, galaxal 901.

**RESPONSE**\
Form R/5\
Reading Room, issued at the counter\
Reference: Request 11-0509\
Issued: click 141, galaxal 901

The request is a search of the request register, class III, for an entry received click 129, galaxal 901, in the name given. The search was made across two volumes. One entry answers the description. Four descriptions are produced and are scheduled below.

The request further asks that the entry be withdrawn, and that the withdrawal not be kept. Both are answered here.

**Withdrawal.** Withdrawal is available and requires no decision. The procedure is at enclosure A. It was completed at the counter on the day of the request and the completed form is at enclosure B. The withdrawal took effect on receipt.

**The withdrawal not being kept.** The retention schedule in force, RS/1, sets retention periods. It specifies no disposal action for the series in which a withdrawal is filed. Enquiry was made of the office that maintains the schedule, and the reply is at enclosure C. The retention schedule is not amended at the counter.

A withdrawal is a record of a request. A record of a request is a holding. The repository holds no procedure for the destruction of a holding, and that is a statement about procedure and not about permission.

**Scope of the search.** A class III search is a search of a series. The series searched is series 2, the request register, at volumes 6 and 11. An item file is not a series and is not searched under this class. Nothing further was searched, and nothing further was requested.

**Fees.** Search, one hour at the class III minimum, at the rate in force. Withdrawal, no fee. Descriptions, no charge, a description not being a copy, and a copy being class II. Receipted at the counter on issue.

Form W/9, revision 4\
Issued by the Standing Regulations Office\
In force from click 12, galaxal 897. Revisions 1 to 3 are held.

1. A requester may withdraw a request at any time before it is answered. No ground need be given and none is assessed.
2. Withdrawal is effected by completing this form at the counter, or by post to the address printed at the foot. A withdrawal by post takes effect on receipt and not on posting.

6. A withdrawal may itself be withdrawn, on this form, by this procedure.
7. No fee is charged for a withdrawal.

Revision 4 substituted annotated for struck at paragraph 4, the register holding no operation named struck. Revisions 1 to 3 are held and are available at item level. The form is reviewed on a schedule of one galaxal.

This form provides for withdrawal. Disposal is not a matter for this form.

**ENCLOSURE C**

**MEMORANDUM**\
Reading Room to Standing Regulations Office\
Reference: Request 11-0509\
Filed: click 140, galaxal 901

A requester asks that a completed withdrawal not be retained. The retention schedule in force sets a retention period for series 4 and specifies no disposal action at its expiry. This office asks which office holds the procedure for the disposal of such a record.

**REPLY**\
Standing Regulations Office to Reading Room\
Reference: as above\
Filed: click 141, galaxal 901

The retention schedule sets retention periods. Where no disposal action is specified, none is specified, and that is not a disposal action of nil.

This office maintains the schedule. It does not hold records and is not competent to act upon one. Where a record is held, the office holding it is the office competent to act upon it, within the procedures available to that office.

A request that the schedule be amended may be made to this office in writing. Such a request is a record and is retained.

For completeness, the schedule is itself a record and is retained. Its superseded revisions are retained. Correspondence in which an amendment was proposed and not made is retained. This office has answered on this point on eleven occasions in the current galaxal, and the eleven replies are retained, and the figure is published quarterly with the rest.
