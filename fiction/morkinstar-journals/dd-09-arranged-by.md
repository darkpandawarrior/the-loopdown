# Arranged By

**REQUEST SLIP**\
Form R/2\
Reading Room counter\
Received: tick 12, click 20, galaxal 904

Requester: Semmi. Class requested: III.

Description of what is sought, in the requester's hand, reproduced as supplied:

> I am a stone dresser and I have six days.
>
> I will describe the sheets first and say why after. The counter says the why is not needed. It is going on the paper anyway.
>
> What I want is every sheet you hold that is a setting out for a stone face. They are all the same animal to look at. A circle drawn at full size, or at a reduction with the reduction written on it. The circle divided. The furrows laid off from a chord and ruled through to the skirt. The draft written small at the outer edge in whatever hand cut it. The count of cracks to the land written at the same edge or on the back. Some are on tracing, and some of those are pricked through where a person has taken the pattern off with a needle. Some are cut out of a bigger sheet and the cut runs through the border.
>
> They will not be filed under any of that. They were made in a works office and a works office files by mill and by job, so a sheet sits under the stone it was cut for and not under the pattern that is on it, and the pattern is the only part I want. The counter says there is no asking for it by the pattern, and that the way to ask is to describe it. So I have described it. I have written this out three times and this is the third, and I have kept the other two in my coat.
>
> ~~I do not know how many there are.~~ I do not know how many there are, and I am not asking anybody to tell me before they look.
>
> Now the why. There are stones at Kelder Reach and nobody there dresses them. The man who did died in the spring and taught nobody, which was not meanness, it was that nobody asked him in time. I am going out on the eighth and I am not coming back. What I know I know off one pair of hands at one mill, and one mill's way of doing a thing is not the whole of the thing. Forty patterns laid along a table would be the whole of it, or near enough that a person could teach off it and not be lying to anybody.
>
> I have thirty for this. I know the copying frame is by the sheet and I cannot have it. I will copy by hand at the table. I will bring my own paper and pencil and be no trouble, and I will work the days you are open before the eighth. If the search comes out at more than two hours, say so at the counter and I will take what two hours buys.

**Second production.** The forty one items produced under this request on click 20 are produced again on this click. Item 12/499/8 is among them and is at the position it carries in the schedule.

A schedule of items produced was written at this production, as one is written at every production. Two schedules exist for this request and they do not differ. The second was made because a second production was made. Both are retained. The repository does not discard a schedule on the grounds that it is duplicated.

No further search was undertaken and no further search fee arises, the items having been identified at the first production.

**The order in which the items were produced.** The request asks under what arrangement the items were produced. The arrangement of a series is stated in the description of that series. The description of series 12 is produced at enclosure A. A description is reproduced as it stands, and a field carrying no value is reproduced carrying no value.

No procedure of this repository prescribes the order in which produced items are placed on a trolley or set out on a table. The production standard, PS/2, requires that every item answering the description is produced, that the items are produced together, and that a schedule is written recording what was produced. It does not prescribe a sequence. No other standing instruction prescribes one. Enquiry was made of the office that maintains the production standard before this paragraph was written, and the reply is filed with this request.

**The note.** A note is not a request. It is filed with the request to which it relates. It is not entered in the register and no fee arises.

Signed for the repository: Reading Services, Search Room. Form R/5, revision 9.
