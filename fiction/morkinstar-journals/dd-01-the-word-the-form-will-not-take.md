[x] I  [ ] II  [ ] III  [ ] IV

Description of the item required. Be as exact as you are able. Match is by description. This office cannot search for what you are not able to describe. Continue overleaf if necessary.

> My grandmother's people had a word for themselves. I have been trying for four momenta to put it on a residency form and the form will not take it. The office at Brack Reach says it is not a permitted value and gave me my papers back with a notice, which I have pinned to this slip, because the woman at the counter said the notice would tell you where to look better than I could, and she was kind about it, and she was right.
>
> ~~I want to say first that I am not accusing anybody of~~ I am not saying anybody has made a mistake. I have read the notice twice. The notice says the permitted list was made out of the first enumeration returns, and that you are the office that holds them. So the word is either in the returns or it is not. If it is not, then that is that, and I will write the nearest one and stop, and I will not be back about it.
>
> If it is there I would like a copy of the line. Not the sheet and not the page around it. The line. I will pay for the sheet it comes on. My cousin had a title deed copied here two years ago and told me what it cost, and I have brought that and a little over in case it has gone up since.
>
> I should say why, in case it changes which drawer you look in. My grandmother's house was let go last winter and I am registering at a new address, and the register entry is what the water connection is assessed on, so this is not a matter of feeling. I have to write something in field six by the end of the period or I am assessed as unregistered and the assessment is higher, and I can pay that, but I would rather not, and I would rather not for the reason you would expect.
>
> I have ticked class one because I thought I was asking for a word off a list. I do not think that is right now that I have written it down. Put me in whatever class this actually is and I will pay whatever that is.
>
> The word is Möndri. The ö carries two dots. That is the whole of the difference, and I would not have come otherwise.

Signed: Tervi. Date: tick 9, momenta 3, click 91, galaxal 884.

Attached by the requester: one notice, Registrations and Residency, Brack Reach, form RR/4c, two sides. Retained on the file.

**NOTICE OF NON-ACCEPTANCE**\
Registrations and Residency, Brack Reach\
Form RR/4c, revision 7\
Reference: application R-88214\
Filed: tick 2, momenta 3, click 91, galaxal 884

Your application for entry in the residency register is returned to you with your papers, all of which are enclosed and none of which is retained. The application is not refused. It is not accepted at the counter, which is a different thing, and the difference is set out below because applicants ask and are entitled to be told.

Field 6 of form RR/1 is the description of persons. Field 6 accepts a value only where that value appears in the Schedule of Permitted Descriptions (Persons), Standing Regulations Office, revision 44.

Value entered at field 6: Möndri\
Nearest value appearing on the schedule: Mondri

An application entering a value that appears on the schedule is accepted at the counter, entered the same tick, and generates no correspondence. An application entering any other value cannot be accepted at the counter. This office holds no discretion in the matter. The discretion was exercised once, at compilation, by the office that compiled the schedule, and it was exercised on typography and not on persons.

You may re-present the application at any counter of this office at any time. There is no fee for re-presentation and no limit on the number of times an application may be re-presented. Your place in the register is not affected by the interval, the register being kept by address and not by date of entry.

Amendment of a permitted value is available and the procedure is short. It is carried out by the Standing Regulations Office under note 9 of the schedule, on the application of a body of record, on production of the source authority for the value, and on payment of the compilation fee. This office is not the source authority and does not hold it.

The schedule states its own source authority at note 2. That note, note 9, and the row bearing the nearest value are reproduced overleaf, so that you are not obliged to buy a copy of the schedule in order to read the three parts of it that concern you.
