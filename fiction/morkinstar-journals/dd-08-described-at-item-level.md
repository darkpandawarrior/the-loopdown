# Described At Item Level

> Request 11-0517 · class II · returned complete

---

**REQUEST SLIP**\
Reading Room. Form R-1, revision 8.\
*Describe the item you require. This office cannot search on subject alone. If the item you require is not described in the catalogue, describe it here in your own words, at whatever length you need.*

Name: Torgir\
Date: tick 39, click 118, galaxal 903\
Class requested: I ☐ · II ☒ · ~~III~~ ☐ · IV ☐

> I came in this morning to file the search and I have not filed it, because I read the board on the way past and the search is charged by the hour with an hour minimum and I would like to know what an hour buys before I buy two of them.
>
> Here is the job. There is a culvert line under the west approach and I am cutting a trench across it in the spring. Nobody has the run of it. The parish had it copied once and the copy is no good, it stops at the third bend and picks up again as somebody else's hand. I have been told the works orders for that road were kept and that you hold them. That is the search I want to file, and I will describe the series properly when I file it, I have the reference off the parish copy.
>
> What I want today is different and it is one sheet if it exists. I have been told you describe yourselves the same as you describe everything else, that there is an entry for the book the entries are in, and that it has an extent on it like a bundle of paper has an extent on it. If that is a real document I will take a copy of it and pay for the sheets.
>
> My reason is only that I have been quoted three hundred and forty for a trial trench, and I can pay two hours here instead, and before I pay two hours here I would like some idea whether this place is a hundred shelves or the size of a town. I am not asking anybody to guess. I am asking for the sheet with the number on it.
>
> I will say plainly that I do not much mind which way it goes. The trench is money and a week and a hole in a road that has to be made good after, and the search is money and an afternoon, and if the search comes out the more expensive of the two then I dig. I have costed worse things than this on less. The one thing I cannot cost is a place I have not been told the size of, and that is why this slip is the shape it is.

**RESPONSE TO REQUEST**\
Reading Room, Retrievals. Form R-3, revision 21.\
Reference: 11-0517\
Issued: tick 41, click 118, galaxal 903

The request describes the repository's own description of itself, at item level, and asks for a copy of it. The repository holds such a description. It is item 1 of series 1. It is described at item level, as every holding in the repository is described at item level.

A copy is enclosed at enclosure A, three sheets. The description cites the notice of the last inventory, and a copy of that notice is enclosed at enclosure B, one sheet, because a figure that is stated as at a date is not complete without the document that fixes the date.

Charges, per the schedule of fees and charges, revision 12. Copying, four sheets at 2 the sheet: 8. No other charge arises. Class II service standard is one momenta. This request is returned in two ticks and is within standard.

The slip carries a class III box, struck through. No class III request is entered and no search has been undertaken. The rate for a class III search is published on the schedule of fees and charges at 11 the hour, minimum one hour, and the schedule is on the board at the counter and may be copied at the sheet rate.

This office does not estimate the hours a search will take. The extent of a series is stated in the description of that series. The rate is published. The multiplication is not a function of a description.

Signed for the repository: Reading Room, Retrievals.

**GENERAL INDEX TO THE HOLDINGS OF THE REPOSITORY**

Reference: 1/1\
Level of description: item\
Dates of creation: from the first inventory. Continuing.

**Extent and physical description.** 4.1 linear. Sheets in fastened volumes, 58 volumes, in a run, on the counter side of the reading room. Volumes 1 to 3 are a fair copy. The volumes they were copied from are described at 1/2 and are retained. The repository does not discard a description on the grounds that it is duplicated. The hand changes at volume 40 and continues.

**Condition.** Sound. Volumes 4 and 5 are rebacked. Volume 40 is worn at the fore edge for the first eleven leaves and the text is not affected.

**Arrangement.** By fonds, thereunder by series, thereunder by item.

**Provenance.** Created by the repository in the course of its function of describing its holdings.

**Scope and content.** The item describes the holdings of the repository. Each holding is described at item level and each description states extent, physical form, condition, arrangement, provenance and access, in that order. Aggregate extent described: 1,206,411.4 linear at the last inventory.

**Note on extent.** The extent stated above is as at the last inventory and is stated as at that date every time it is stated. The extent stated is not the extent at time of reading, and no procedure exists that would make it so. The figure was taken at the last inventory and does not include the record of the inventory.

**Access.** No restriction. The item is in continuous use at the counter and is not produced to the reading room. A copy of any entry in it is produced on production of a description, at the sheet rate.

**Custodial history.** The item has not left the custody of the repository. Volumes 1 to 3 were copied at the counter and the counter is in the repository.

**Appraisal.** No appraisal is applied to this item and none is applied to any holding. No procedure exists for the destruction of a holding, and no procedure exists for the destruction of a description of a holding.

**Accruals.** Expected. Accruals are described at the inventory following.

**Language and script.** The language of the descriptions is the language of the office that made them. Where a holding is in another language the description states the language and does not translate the holding.

**Rules and conventions.** Descriptions are made to the standing description rule, revision 6, which requires extent and physical form before subject in every entry.

**Dates of description.** Each entry carries the date it was made. Entries amended at an inventory carry the date of the amendment and the date of the entry before amendment, and both entries are retained.

**Related material.** Notice of the last inventory, at 2/14. The schedule of fees and charges, revision 12, at 2/9. The standing description rule, revision 6, at 2/1.
