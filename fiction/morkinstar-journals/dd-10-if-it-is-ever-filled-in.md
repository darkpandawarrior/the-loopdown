**MEMORANDUM OF CLASSING**\
Reading Room, Retrievals\
Form R/9, revision 14\
Reference: request 11-0544\
Issued: tick 121, click 22, galaxal 884

The description at field 5 of the slip refers to the length. Field 3 of the slip cites request 11-0463 of this register, which was a class I retrieval of a published schedule, produced complete, in the same tick, at no charge, and which named the interval and the column. The two fields together are sufficient to match and no search was required to establish it. Nothing in this memorandum reaches past the description given on the slip.

Class is assigned on what is asked for. The four classes are stated on the retrieval standards schedule, revision 12, and each is a class of document, that being what the classes were made to describe. They are considered here in order, because a request that is not in one of them is only in the residual once the four have been considered.

Class I is a value from a published schedule. The request does not ask for a value. It asks about the entering of a value that has not been entered, and a value that has not been entered is not a value on a schedule.

Class II is a copy of a held document. The request states that no copy is required, and the requester repeated that at the counter, and in any case the repository holds no document that answers the description, this office holding documents and not intentions to produce them.

Class III is a search across a series. A search is a search of holdings. Holdings are described as they stand at the date of the description. No search across any series produces a document that does not exist at the date the search is made.

Class IV is a certified copy for evidential use. Certification is applied to a copy. There is no copy.

The request is therefore classed in the class not assigned, that being the class the schedule provides for anything the four above do not describe. It is a printed row on a published schedule and it carries a service standard, as the four rows above it do.

The service standard for the class not assigned is the residual interval, stated on the schedule as one Elysheim, and reproduced here as it is printed:

> **RETRIEVAL STANDARDS SCHEDULE**, revision 12. Extract, final row only. The rows above this one are omitted from this extract and are available in full as a class I retrieval, same tick, no charge, to anybody who asks at the counter.
>
> | Class | Request | Service standard | Fee basis |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | not assigned | anything the four classes above do not describe | one Elysheim | no basis stated |
>
> *Note 7. The standard for each class is stated by reference to an interval of the Standard Intervals, Section 3 of the founding charter. Where an interval is cited the standard is the interval as it stands on that schedule at the date the request is received.*

The standard is met. It has been met since the tick of receipt and is met at the date of this memorandum.

Two procedures are described here because they are the two a requester most often asks for at this stage, and both exist, and both are published.

Escalation. A request may be escalated to the Standing Regulations Office where the service standard for its class has been breached. The application is made on form R/12, requires the register number and the date on which the standard was breached, and attracts no fee. No standard has been breached in respect of this request. The date required by the form does not arise and the form does not accept the field left empty.

Closure. A request is closed on the register when it has been returned, the register recording the disposition and the date of return in the same entry. There is no procedure for closing a request that has not been returned, no such procedure having been required.

On the matter the request describes. A schedule is amended by revision. A revision is a document, and every revision of every published schedule is held by this repository, and any revision of any published schedule is produced to any person on application at the counter, in the same tick, at no charge, that being a class I retrieval and the cheapest service this office provides. The superseded revision is retained and is produced on the same terms. No procedure requires this office to inform a person that a revision has been made, the distribution list for a revision being a list of offices, and a person not being an office. No person is prevented from applying, and no application is refused, and there is no limit on the number of times a person may apply.

The request asks to be told. Telling is not a document. The repository produces documents on production of a description. The request is received, date stamped, entered in the register, and classed.

Issued for the repository: Reading Room, Retrievals. A form of this class is issued without signature.
