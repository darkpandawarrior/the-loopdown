# Build Log

One session. One 2021 short story in, three seasons and a published site out. This records
what was decided, what was killed, what it cost, and what was learned, because most of it is
only useful if it survives the session.

**Total measured external spend: ~$3.03.** Everything else ran on the subscription.

---

## What existed at the start

`archive/legend-of-koaeluae-scales.md`. One story, 1,804 words, written 2021. It already had the
whole frame in it and did not know it was a series: a Galactic Directory correspondent filing
numbered journal entries about why each world has the weather it has. Fourteen gods, fourteen
monsters, a serpent who splits himself in two, snow as the leftover.

## The decisions that mattered

### 1. The count of fourteen was already in the 2021 story

Not invented. The source says *"There were 14 gods and 14 monsters"* in passing, as scenery. The
entire series is the consequence of taking one throwaway line literally.

### 2. The milgalaxal error is inherited, not written

The 2021 glossary says a milgalaxal is 2228 Hellheims **or 2455 Earth years**, while a click is
1 Hellheim is 2 Earth years. That does not multiply out. Rather than correct it, Entry #2300 is
built on it: the Hellheim was rebased when the world it was keyed to was Concluded, every line in
the table was restated, and that one was missed. **By being missed it became the last surviving
record of that world's year.** He spends four hundred entries trying to get it deleted.

The single best thing in the anthology came from refusing to fix a typo.

### 3. Season 1's second pass: the wrong hypothesis

The first draft had him notice the 14/14 count and simply tally it for eight entries, so the
reader was ahead of him with nothing to do. Fixed by giving him **the Template Hypothesis**: the
count is contamination, the Directory printed it onto worlds that never had it. He names it,
states its own disproof condition, walks into that condition on Jötunheimr, patches it weaker,
watches the patch die on Cendre, and buries it on Solvei.

He is right that it is a Directory artifact. He has the arrow backwards. **He tested it on the
gods and never on the bodies**, which is what makes the species work in this same session land.

### 4. Season 2's spine came from a cross-lab note

The first S2 slate was ten premises and a rising number. A seven-lab audit called the number
*"a countdown widget, not a spine"* and then gave the season away:

> *"Cendre burns its archive because the monster IS the archive. The narrator is building his own
> archive. Each page he writes should make him MORE Concluded, not less."*

Built entirely out of canon already on the page. He transcribes Skerrin's rule in #2284 and then
starts an archive anyway. The rising Concluded count stops being weather and becomes his fault.

### 5. Season 3 v1 was killed outright

The first S3 design was fourteen entries by fourteen different authors writing into his case.
Killed by cross-lab on three counts, all structural:

- **Pre-owned.** *"Literally the margin-notes premise of S. / Ship of Theseus."* Plus *Hyperion*,
  *World War Z*, *Illuminae*, *House of Leaves*.
- **It defanged the monster.** Many hands means the archive stops being a self-portrait, so
  Skerrin cannot eat it. *"If the solution to your cosmic horror is get your friends to co-sign
  your diary, you have utterly defanged the central threat."* The season was a loophole.
- **It traded one fatigue for a worse one.** A thousand words per narrator is not enough to care
  about anyone, and it discards the asset: readers have become co-conspirators in his cadence.

The replacement came from re-reading canon and finding I had the Væhn ritual backwards. **They are
not safe because many children write the pages. They are safe because they burn everything.** He
copied the slots and the never-written rule and left behind the only part that works.

### 6. The Rendering, which reframed everything

Discovered while fixing the artwork, and it is now series-level canon.

Every description in thirty entries arrived through **a translation rig and a Directory form**.
*"Bipedal category species"* is not an observation, it is a checkbox. **Nobody in this anthology
has ever been seen.** These are renderings, and a rendering can hold, strain, fail, or refuse.

Three consequences:
- Variance between plates is canon, not inconsistency.
- **Ossul is a refusal.** His species is never named in thirty entries, so the instrument does not
  get to invent one. His plate draws his hands, his lamp and his second chair in full detail
  while the body will not come.
- **The correspondent has no body.** Never described, never given a species. Everyone else at
  least got a rendering. He did not. **The only thing he is, is the account** — which is the
  engine Season 3 was missing.

### 7. The medium

Radio-show insight, resolved along a fact already true: **Season 1 was broadcast, Season 2 was
never sent.** So S1 reading pages carry relay chrome, S2 carry none, and the absence is what a
reader notices. S3 is neither: those pages no longer exist, so it is scorched.

Artifacts only where the fiction says something is damaged. Entry #2300 does not merely stop, the
transmission terminates. Tape stays monospace and cannot lie; testimony stays sans and can.

### The ownership test, which is the reusable method

Send the premises to seven labs with **no context about what inspired anything** and ask them to
name the source. If a different training family names it unaided, ownership failed. It cannot be
self-assessed: an author cannot see what they metabolised, and a Claude panel shares too much of
my distribution to detect it.

Useful negative control: **nobody named Assassination Classroom for the god who announced his own
end date.** The gate detects real borrowing rather than pattern-matching everything.

## What was learned about the tools

1. **`openrouter ask` cannot return an image.** It is a text interface; image models put the
   result in `choices[].message.images[].image_url.url` and leave `content` empty, so it bills the
   generation and prints nothing. Measured: **$0.0672 for an empty string.** Now the first warning
   in the `image-generation` skill.
2. **Token math does not predict image cost.** Image models bill per image. `gemini-3-pro-image`
   cost **$0.1401 for one image against a $0.015 token-based estimate**, 9x. Quote from a measured
   per-image table.
3. **Probe one, look at it, then draw the rest.** Both portrait sets were probed first. The second
   probe is what proved the doctrine worked before spending $1.26.
4. **Reasoning models starve at low `--max`.** `kimi-k2-thinking` and `glm-5.2` returned empty
   while billing the full cap, repeatedly.
5. Both are now encoded in `openrouter-image` and the `image-generation` skill, which are general
   and not specific to this project.

## Bugs found, and the two that were mine twice

Nine issues in the live-site audit, all fixed. The two worth remembering:

**Markdown tables rendered as raw pipes.** No `remark-gfm`. Three entries carry tables and in two
of them *the table is the entry*: page thirty is a weighing whose whole point is a difference
column that climbs, and page ninety-one is the charter schedule where an interval that never had
a length finally has one. Both shipped as one mangled line. This is the anthology's **only** new
dependency and it was not optional.

**`fiction/**` was missing from the CI path filters.** `build-registry.mjs` was extended to scan
the anthology, but the trigger was not, so editing a story ran no staleness check and two commits
landed with a stale registry. **The workflow file already documented this exact trap for
`archive/**`**, at length, and I read it, extended the generator, and made the identical mistake
one directory over on the same day.

*The rule is about every directory the generator reads, not the one that taught it.*

## Process notes

- **A second Claude session was live in `cv-siddharth` throughout.** Both PRs were built in
  isolated **git worktrees** off `origin/main` rather than switching branches under a live
  session. Their tree was never touched and both merged clean.
- **I merged PR #27 before Lighthouse finished**, expecting `--auto` to gate on it. It is not a
  required check on that repo. It passed anyway; the mistake was relying on `--auto` without
  checking what was actually required.
- **Verify delegated claims.** The cross-lab called the Solvei arithmetic broken. It was not: 13
  splits give 26 names, the unsplit one holds a god-name and an unnamed monster line, which is
  exactly 14/14 with thirteen names and a pause. The real defect was that the story never showed
  its working. A voice audit also reported a "16x drop" in a metric that measured as 3.9x.
- **An agent reported a phantom bug of mine**: my own audit script's selector found zero starmap
  labels when all eighteen were present. A false positive in an audit costs as much as a miss.

## Things that look like mistakes and are load-bearing

Anyone touching this repo should read these before "fixing" something.

1. **The milgalaxal conversion does not multiply out.** Inherited from 2021. Entry #2300 is built
   on it. It is the last surviving measurement of a Concluded world.
2. **Entry #2300 ends mid-sentence.** Filed incomplete on purpose. Never finish it.
3. **Season 2 never uses the word "concluding".** Season 1's sign-off did. That is the season.
4. **The plates are visually inconsistent between seasons and between species.** That is The
   Rendering. See `species.md`.
5. **Season 3's visible burns are a selection**, not all ninety. Most of the case empties
   off-page. Seeded early on purpose.

## The art revamp, and the two defects that survived a verify pass

The last pass composited the tellers **into** the plates, dropped the sigils back to large faint
watermarks, banded Season One as a received relay transmission, and added a registration mark.
An automated verify agent found and fixed four collisions (Feeriko over the s1-01 clock circle,
the Cendran Child over the s1-07 page box, a z-order bug where s2-04's panel painted over
Hallovar, Ossul over the s2-01 chairs) and declared s1-02 clean.

**s1-02 was not clean, and the verifier was not entirely wrong either.** Cropped and upscaled,
Tveggi is perfectly legible at full resolution — a kneeling figure, a stone, a burnt stick. But
the portrait terminated in a hard rectangle, and the site shows these as thumbnail cards, where
a hard-edged warm rectangle on a dark plate reads as a pasted grey box. The verifier checked
legibility. The defect was in the edges.

The cause is the kind that survives review because the code says the right thing:

- `witness()` **already had** a radial feather mask. It faded nothing. The gradient ran
  `r="0.6"` with its transparent stop at `1.0`, and a `radialGradient` in `objectBoundingBox`
  units is an **ellipse matched to the box** — `r=0.5` only just reaches the edge midpoints and
  the corners sit out near `0.707`. The mask was still fully opaque where the rectangle ended.
  Stops now finish at `0.44`.

The second defect is the mirror image — a deliberate choice taken one step too far:

- Plate 14 is the one undamaged object in Season Three and it carried **the one unreadable
  label in the season**. `KEPT` was drawn in the entry's accent at 0.8 opacity on pale paper,
  about **1.4:1**. Every `WITHDRAWN` stamp reads fine because it uses solid ink-weight red.
  Quiet is a matter of *treatment*, not of contrast: the badge now takes its calm from what it
  does not do — no rotation, no heavy rule, no red — and keeps the accent as a bar down the left
  edge, where colour decorates rather than carries the word.

Both shipped in `3bac146`, and `cv-siddharth` PR #30 regenerates against that commit so the
site gets the fixed renders rather than the pre-fix ones.

### The lesson worth keeping

Twice now the failure has been **an intention encoded correctly and executed into nothing**: a
frontmatter parser that checked for an array after the quotes had been stripped, a strip regex
that left a blank line so `^#` never matched, and now a feather whose gradient never reached the
edge. In all three the code reads as if it works. Only the artifact says otherwise. **Look at
the output, at the size it will actually be seen.**
