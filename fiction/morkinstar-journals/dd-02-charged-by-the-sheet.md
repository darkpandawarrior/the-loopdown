**REQUEST SLIP**\
Reading Services, Public Counter\
Form Q/1, revision 12\
Request 11-0452. Received: click 94, galaxal 884.

Name of requester: Orven\
Date: click 94, galaxal 884

Class requested, tick one: I ☐ · II ☒ · III ☐ · IV ☐

*Describe what you are asking for. Use the back of the sheet if you need it.*

> My eastern boundary is not where my deeds say it is. It is out by about the width of my hand, and it is out by the same amount the whole way along, so it is not the stone leaning and it is not me. I have walked it twice with a line.
>
> I am selling the top half of the parcel to pay for something that is my own business. The buyer's surveyor will not put his name to the plan until somebody says which of the two papers is the right one. I have been told that is not his job to say and I believe him.
>
> I went to the registry at Coombe first. The man there was good to me. He got the sheet out and put it on the counter next to my deeds and the two of them do not agree, and he could not tell me why, and he did not pretend that he could. He said the paper behind the sheet comes here.
>
> The stone is my father's stone. He set it and I have re-bedded it twice and both times I put it back in its own hole, and the hole is where he cut it. I say that because I know what it sounds like when a man says his boundary has shifted, and I would say the same thing in your place.
>
> So I would like to know when it moved and who moved it. ~~I am not saying anybody~~ I am not accusing anybody of anything. I would like a date I can write on the form.
>
> I will pay what it costs.
>
> Orven

The request as filed is marked class II. Class is assigned on what is requested and not on how the request is marked. A copy of a document cited by the requester is class II. The fee basis for class III is per hour, minimum one hour, and is posted at the public counter. Copies are charged by the sheet at the rate posted beside it.

Match in this repository is by description. The description supplied is sufficient. It gives a cadastral sheet, a reach, a boundary marker recoverable from the sheet, and a recorded offset. No further description was required and none was requested.

Four items answer the description. One further document is reproduced because the item that answers the description reproduces it. All five are enclosed, copied complete, at the extents stated below.

The repository holds no record of any mark being moved on Cadastral Sheet 12. The repository holds no series in which such a record would be filed.
