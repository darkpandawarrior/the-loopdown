# The Shortest Page In The Case

> Kindling · page 34 withdrawn

Page thirty four, my dear readers, is not an entry. It is a sentence, and then, underneath the
sentence, in smaller writing, an apology for the sentence being so short. I do not remember which
world I was on. I do not remember why I stopped there and did not go on to make it a proper entry
with a Fun Fact and a disclaimer and all the rest of the furniture. I only have what is on the page,
which is this:

*The blind fish in the flooded temple still turn toward the door when it opens.*

And under that, in the smaller hand: *I am sorry this is so short. I looked at it again and there
did not seem to be anything to add that would not make it worse.*

He was right, whoever he was that day. There is nothing to add. I have tried, sitting here, to
think of the Fun Fact that would have gone with it (Fun Blind Fish Fact: they cannot see the door
either) and it is a bad joke and I know it is a bad joke while I am making it, which did not used
to happen quite this fast.

Anyway. It is one sentence. It goes in the fire the way a sentence goes in a fire, which is to say
all at once, no lingering, no second look, because there is nothing on the page to linger over
that I have not already told you.

There. Page thirty four withdrawn.

That is the whole entry. I am aware it is the whole entry. Eighty six to go, and some of them
will take longer than this took.
