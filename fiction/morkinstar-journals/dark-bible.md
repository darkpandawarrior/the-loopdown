# The Dark Directory

## Season One: Requests Received

Ten retrieval files. Nine requesters. One index that has never once been wrong.

A sibling anthology. **It shares a universe and it does not share a cast.** A reader who has read
the parent should feel the join in about four places and never be told where. A reader who has not
read a word of it must lose nothing at all, and that is a hard requirement rather than a courtesy:
if a piece needs the parent to work, the piece is broken and the fix is in the piece.

> **On this file's own status.** This is a working bible, which means it is a spoiler index and a
> kill record, and under the leak doctrine's one way door it is barred from every fiction surface
> and no fiction page links here. It names the parent's proper nouns in exactly one place, the
> never list, in order to forbid them. Nowhere else in this file, and nowhere at all in a piece.

---

## THE THESIS (this is the series, and nothing may contradict it)

An archive answers requests. It holds more than its catalogue lists, and not because anything is
hidden: listing requires a field, some things have no field, and a holding with no catalogue path
can be reached only by somebody who can already describe it. The office that answers is not a
person. It is a document. A general index, describing its own holdings, which never states anything
false and is not the kind of thing that can be right about itself, because its own extent is one of
its holdings and writing the extent down adds a holding. Around it, procedure executes. A world
somewhere is Concluded, a Standard value keyed to it is rebased, every value derived from it is
restated, and because **scope is a derived value** the edge of the archive passes over things
nobody deposited. Nothing was admitted. Nothing acts. Nine people ask nine reasonable questions,
receive nine complete and correct answers, and not one of them finds out what they came to find out.

That paragraph is the whole design. If a drafter can hold it, they can write a piece.

### The flip, stated once, because it is the reason this exists

The parent runs an **honest narrator and an unreliable record**. A man measures, files corrections
nobody actions, and everything reaches you through an instrument that may hold, strain or fail.
`error-doctrine.md` names it exactly: *"The anthology does not have an unreliable narrator. It has
an honest narrator and an unreliable record, and that is a stronger machine, but it is also a
machine that will happily launder a typo."*

This series runs it the other way. **The record is impeccable and the narrator cannot be trusted**,
and the narrator cannot be trusted for one reason only, which is that the narrator is not a person
and is therefore out of scope for itself. It never lies. It has no motive to. It has no motive at
all. It is a finding aid, and a finding aid that describes what it holds will eventually describe
something it should not be able to hold, and will describe it correctly, in the same flat register
it uses for a folded sheet, and will move on to the next series.

**The unreliability is scope, never accuracy.** Any piece that makes the index wrong about a fact
has broken the series and does not get patched, it gets rewritten.

## THE MEDIUM

### What a document in this series physically is

**A retrieval file.** Not a story about an archive. The paper one transaction generated, fastened
in the order it was generated in, and kept because keeping it is the procedure.

The reader is not being told about a request. The reader is holding the file, after the fact,
in the order an office would have left it: slip on top because it was filed first, response under
it, enclosures under that, the retrieval note at the bottom because it was written last. **The
reader arrives mid transaction**, at a request number that is not one and inside a fee period that
started long before them, and nothing greets them, because a file has no greeting.

The parent's four media are four relations between a record and its reader: broadcast, never sent,
destroyed, executed. This is the fifth and it is the only one where **somebody asked**. Season Four
posts to everybody and is read by nobody. This publishes nothing at all and is read by one person
at a time, on request, at a fee, in a room.

### The first line

The parent's four:

```
> Entry #2245 · Series 4 of 16, Alpha Axmoiri
> Page 7 of 91
> Kindling · page 7 withdrawn
> Posted · THE STEPPED QUARTER · cleared in 24 ticks
```

This one:

```
> Request 11-0447 · class II · returned complete
```

Three parts and all three are load bearing.

**The request number**, because it says a reader has walked in on something. Period eleven, sequence
0447. There were four hundred and forty six before this one in this period alone and the series will
never mention a single one of them.

**The retrieval class**, because the class carries the service standard, and the service standard is
where this series keeps its cosmology. Class is assigned on what is being asked for, never on who is
asking. There is no priority. There is no queue jumping. There is nobody to ask.

**The disposition**, and here is the joke that runs the whole season: **it reads `returned complete`
in nine pieces out of ten.** The index does not fail. Not once. Nine complete, correct, timely
returns, and nine people who did not get what they came for.

The tenth piece's header has **two parts**, because the third field is not blank, it does not exist:

```
> Request 11-0544 · class not assigned
```

That is not an error and it is not the season's one class 4 error either (see **Custody**, below).
It is a correct header for a request that has no class, written by an office that would never print
a field it has no value for.

### The retrieval classes

| Class | What it is a request for | Service standard | Fee basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | A value from a published schedule | Same tick | No charge |
| **II** | A copy of a held document | One momenta | Per sheet |
| **III** | A search across a series | One click | Per hour, minimum one |
| **IV** | A certified copy for evidential use | One click, plus certification | Per sheet, plus the certificate |
| **not assigned** | Anything the four above do not describe | The residual interval | No basis |

Four classes and a residual. **The residual class is Elysheim and this is the only place the series
gets to spend it.** Section 3 of the founding charter names eight intervals and leaves two with no
length. The retrieval standards schedule cites intervals, the way the charter does, and the residual
standard cites the one that has never been assigned one.

So a request in the residual class has a published service standard, is fully compliant at all
times, cannot be late, cannot be escalated because escalation is a procedure for a standard that has
been breached, and cannot be answered. **It is not backlogged. Its standard is met continuously.**

The parent's never list is explicit and this obeys it to the letter: *"Never give Elysheim a length.
It has read not yet required since the founding charter."* Nothing here assigns one. A realm with no
duration is a place where nothing takes time, so a queue keyed to it never advances and never
stalls, and the requests in it are neither pending nor answered. **No piece may improve on that
sentence, locate that queue, count its position, or describe where a request in it has gone. It has
not gone anywhere. There is no anywhere.**

### The furniture every piece carries

Five things, always, in this order. A piece missing any of them is not finished.

1. **The header line**, exactly as above.
2. **The slip, reproduced verbatim**, in the requester's own words, including the crossings out, the
   second thoughts and the wrong box ticked. The requester's name and the date and nothing else
   about them. **This is the only place a person speaks in this entire series** and it is where all
   the warmth lives.
3. **At least one document produced by an office**, correctly formatted, carrying a form number, a
   filing reference and a date. Real administrative prose. Somebody was paid to write it.
4. **The index's response**, in the index's voice, which is defined below and is not negotiable.
5. **The retrieval note** at the foot, always in this order and this flatness:

```
Holding cited: [fonds / series / item, or the statement that no call number exists]
Extent and condition: [linear extent, physical form, condition term]
Access: [two sentences, both true, and they will not sound compatible]

Requests in class not assigned: 4,112
Found in collection at last inventory: 61

This description is of the holding. It is not a copy of the holding.
This finding aid describes the holdings of the repository. It does not describe the repository.
```

**Those last two lines are printed in full at the foot of all ten pieces and are never remarked
on.** Season Four printed a young man's sincere sentence at the foot of fourteen pieces as a
licensing requirement and never let him look at it until piece thirteen. This is the same device
and it is aimed at the narrator instead of the correspondent. The second line is the entire flip,
in the small print, in the register of an access statement, ten times, and nobody in the series is
capable of reading it, because the only reader on the archive's side is the finding aid.

### A piece is more than one document

**Minimum two documents, maximum five, and at least one written by a person and one by an office.**

This is the structural rule and it is the one that keeps the form alive. A piece that is one
document is a monologue, and a monologue by an index is a lecture. The transaction is the form. The
existing pilot, `pilot-the-restatement.md`, is four documents and a reproduced standing order in
726 words, and it works because the reader is made to do the joining.

**Length: 1,100 to 1,600 words a piece, about 13,000 for the season.** Deliberately under the
parent's, and the reason is the medium and not laziness. There is no digression here, no
parenthetical, no direct address, no aside, no charm. Every word is on a document, and padding a
document is the one operation that stops it being a document. If a piece is running short, the fix
is another document, never a longer paragraph.

### The pilot, and where it goes

`pilot-the-restatement.md` is not entry one. It has no requester and no index in it. **It is the
prologue, and it is also the enclosure to entry two.** A parcel holder asks when his eastern
boundary moved, and what the index returns is those four documents: the closure notice, Standing
Order Six reproduced, the rebasing worksheet, the cadastral restatement, and the filing note that
ends *"No record was kept of which."*

That is not a retrofit for tidiness. It gives every drafter a worked example, at full size, of what
a complete return looks like in this series, and it makes the prologue load bearing instead of
orphaned.

## THE VOICE

The index is the narrator. Working out what that sounds like is the single most important craft
problem in the series, and getting it wrong in either of the two obvious directions kills it.

**It is not a person.** No first person, anywhere, ever. It does not say *I*. It says *this office*
when it acts and *the repository* when it describes. There is no clerk. There is no name on a
response and there never will be. Responses are signed with a post title and a form number.

**It is not a computer.** No systems, no queries, no processing, no interface, no beeping, no
retrieval engine. Nothing in this series is electronic. It is paper, a schedule, a fee, a stamp and
a room.

**It is not spooky.** It never portends. It never trails off. It never says *however* in a way that
means *beware*. It has no atmosphere. Anything that reads as atmosphere in a finished piece is the
reader's, supplied for free by the flatness, and the moment the prose asks for it the effect is
gone.

**It is a finding aid.** Which means, precisely:

- It states **extent and condition before subject**, always. What a thing physically is comes before
  what it says. That is the register's fingerprint and it is the fastest way to get the voice right.
- It **answers the question asked** and not the question meant, and it does not notice the
  difference, because noticing is not a function of a description.
- It says no by **describing what is available instead**, never by declining. There is no refusal in
  this series. There are complete answers to slightly different questions.
- It **never apologises, never regrets, never reassures, never hopes.** Cut *unfortunately*,
  *sadly*, *we are pleased*, *please note*, *kindly*, *at this time*.
- It **repeats a term rather than finding a synonym.** Variation is a courtesy to a reader and this
  is not addressed to a reader.
- It **states two things that do not sound compatible** and does not join them, because joining them
  would be an inference and inference is out of scope.

### Four sentences to imitate

> The series consists of forty one items, 0.3 linear extent, sound. It is arranged by designation,
> thereunder by date of closure. Item 17 is a single sheet folded twice, and the fold is described
> here because it is the only feature the item has.

> No restriction applies. Retrieval is not available. These are separate statements and both are
> current.

> The request asks for the length of the interval. The repository holds the schedule on which the
> interval is listed. The schedule is produced in full. The column is present.

> Two descriptions of this item exist and they do not differ. The second was made because the first
> could not be found at the time. Both are retained. The repository does not discard a description
> on the grounds that it is duplicated.

And one for the self description, which is the hardest register in the series and appears in full
only once:

> The finding aid is item 1 of series 1 and is described at item level. Its extent is stated as at
> the last inventory. The extent stated is not the extent at time of reading, and no procedure
> exists that would make it so.

### And one sentence to imitate from the other side

The slips are where the series is warm, and the warmth is canon law carried over intact:
`bible.md`'s *"Warmth, never contempt"*. **No requester is ever a fool, ever a punchline, ever
wrong to ask.** Their handwriting is the best thing in the file.

> I am not after anything clever. My grandmother's people had a word for themselves and the form
> will not take it. I have been told this office holds the original returns. If you hold it I will
> take a copy of the line and nothing else, and I will pay for the sheet.

## THE LAWS

Eight. The parent has seven and this is not a competition. Each of these is doing work no other one
does, and each is checkable against a finished piece.

**1. The record is impeccable.** Nothing the index states is false. No piece may make it wrong about
a fact, a date, an extent, a condition or a citation. Its returns are complete, correct and on time.
The horror is not that it is mistaken. The horror is that it is not.

**2. The index is out of scope for itself.** It describes every holding correctly, including itself,
and its self description is correct as at the last inventory and cannot be correct at time of
reading, because reading is a request, and a request is a record, and a record is a holding.
**Nothing in the series notices this.** The index cannot notice it, because noticing is inference. No
requester may notice it out loud. It is stated once, flatly, in piece eight, as an ordinary caveat
on an extent figure, and then it is printed at the foot of every piece as boilerplate that is true.

**3. You can only retrieve what you can already describe.** An unlisted holding has no catalogue
path, so it cannot be browsed to, stumbled on, or found by asking around. Match is by description.
**Therefore every slip is a statement of what the requester already knew, and the index's answer
never reaches past the requester's own description.** This is the law that stops the series being
about forbidden knowledge. Nobody is kept out. The archive gives nothing away, to anyone, ever, and
it is not withholding, it is a property of how retrieval works.

**4. Access is a derived value.** Thresholds are keyed to Standard values, Standard values are keyed
to designations, designations receive the Concluded flag, and Standing Order Six restates every
value derived from them at any remove. Collecting scope is defined against a Standard extent, so
restating the Standard restates the scope. The edge of the archive moves. Every step is correct.
Nobody aims it.

**5. Nothing is admitted and nothing is acquired.** New holdings arrive by exactly two routes: a
restatement brought them inside a threshold, or the annual inventory recorded them as **found in
collection**. There is never a donor, never a seizure, never a bequest, never a discovery, never an
entity handing anything over. Nothing was put there. The edge passed over it.

**6. A refusal is never a refusal.** The index does not withhold. It produces exactly what was asked
for, in full, and what was asked for is smaller than what was wanted, and that gap is the piece.
Every *no* in this series is a complete and correct answer to a question adjacent to the one the
requester meant.

**7. Nothing acts.** No being, no agent, no will, no plan, no intention, no arrival. The parent
states the law and the sibling inherits it whole: *"What never acts is the apparatus. Nothing is
done to a world, it is simply finished, and Concluded is an absence of action rather than a
verdict."* Order without an orderer. Growth without a grower. An edge that moves with nobody moving
it. Anything in a draft that wants something is cut, and it is cut rather than softened.

**8. The evidence is order, and order only.** The thing this series is actually about is not a
holding, has no entry, no call number, no rank and no line, because no form has a field for it. It
appears in the series in exactly one way: **a return arrives sorted by a criterion nobody
specified.** That is all. It is never described, never named, never inferred on the page, never
theorised about by a requester, and never denied. **The moment a piece supplies a cause, the series
is finished**, because a cause makes it a thing with a file, and a thing with a file has a line on a
list, and a line on a list is the parent's fourteenth, not this.

## THE ELDRITCH, AND WHY IT HAS NO LINE

The parent's turn is that every world reports fourteen gods and fourteen monsters, thirteen monster
names and a pause, and the pause is the most consistent phenomenon in the anthology. The unnamed
fourteenth has no name. **It still has a line on a list.** The form has fourteen fields, the
fourteenth is printed grey with a keyline at exactly the same size as the other thirteen, and the
absence therefore has dimensions and a reader can measure it.

This series turns on the thing that has no line at all, because there is no form it could be on.
**The thing that does the filing.**

It is not on the god list and not on the monster list. It is not out of scope, because out of scope
is a scope decision. It is not unlisted, because unlisted is a catalogue state and a catalogue state
is recorded. It has no provenance, because provenance is how a thing came in and it did not come in.
It has no extent, because extent is measured and measuring produces a figure and a figure is a
record. There is nothing to withhold and nothing to reveal.

**What is available to a drafter, and this is the entire budget for the season:**

- A return arrives ordered.
- The arrangement statement for that series is blank. Not *arrangement: none*, which is a value.
  Blank, with the field label printed and nothing after it, which is `error-doctrine.md`'s custody
  class 3 exactly: *"An error is legible as an error when the container still shows the missing
  thing's size."*
- No procedure produces that order, and the index says so, because saying so is a description of a
  procedure and descriptions of procedures are in scope.
- The order is reproducible. Ask again and it comes back the same.

Four facts. **No piece may state more than two of them, and only piece nine may state all four.** No
piece relates any of them. No requester connects any of them. The index cannot connect them because
connecting is inference.

**And the hard prohibitions, which get a piece rejected outright:**

- **Never name it.** Not in a piece, not in a plate, not in a caption, not in a blurb, not in a
  filename.
- **Never have anything reject a name for it.** Rejection is engagement and engagement is
  canonisation by other means. The only safe answer to *is it X* is no answer at all. This is the
  parent's standing doctrine on the fourteenth and it is imported without amendment.
- **Never give it a rung, a tier, a rank, a sequence, a pathway or a degree.** The parent's council
  refused this once already and wrote the refusal down so it could not be rediscovered as a fresh
  idea: *"Make it a ladder and you have supplied the one thing the whole work is built on not
  having, which is an institution with somewhere to get to."* A ladder implies a climber, and a
  climber inside the apparatus is the agency four seasons have spent refusing.
- **Never let it act, arrive, notice, respond, choose or want.**
- **Never let it be a growth in mass or weight.** That belongs to the parent, it has a name there,
  and reusing the shape as tonnage is importing a monster with the label filed off. What grows here
  is a **count of descriptions of things nobody deposited**, which is a different object and a
  colder one.

## THE HIERARCHY AS IT NOW STANDS

Stated as procedure, because there is no event to state. Nothing happened. Everything is in force.

**Standing Order Six is executed as written.** Where a designation receives the Concluded flag, any
Standard value keyed to its period, mass or extent is identified, rebased against an active
reference, and republished within one galaxal, and every value derived from it at any remove is
restated and republished before the rebasing takes effect. A value not rebased within the period
lapses and may not be relied upon.

**A restatement is arithmetic and takes effect on posting.** No survey is conducted. No mark is
moved. This is on the page already, in the pilot, in the register of a parcel office: *"The
amendment is arithmetic and takes effect on posting."*

**A restatement has no materiality floor.** There is no rule requiring a derived value to be
significant before it restates. A Span moves by thirty eight parts per million and every class
boundary built on it moves by the same proportion, and the worksheet recomputes without remainder,
and the objection period closes with nothing filed.

**Scope is a derived value, so the archive's edge is a number keyed to a world.** This is the whole
engine and it is the only genuinely new mechanism the sibling adds. Retention scope is defined
against a Standard extent. Concluding a world somewhere restates that extent. Holdings do not enter
the repository. **The repository's edge passes over them**, and they are described at the next
inventory, and their provenance reads found in collection, because nobody deposited them and the
form has a value for exactly that.

**A restatement is not notified to the holding**, because a record is not a party and there is no
procedure for serving notice on a document.

**Custody has never changed hands.** No office has gained authority over another. The relative
standing of every office is exactly as chartered and the chart is published and anybody may see it.
Anyone who says the hierarchy has been altered is wrong, on the record, and can be shown the chart,
and will be, politely, in about six minutes.

**And the hierarchy is not what it was, and both of those are true.** Nobody is lying. Nobody
gained. Arithmetic is not a jurisdiction. What the restatements have done is move where the edges
fall, continuously, in every direction at once, for as long as worlds keep being finished, which is
forever, and the rate is not published because it is not a workload figure. **The number of
restatements is published, quarterly, per office, because that one is.**

### What a new power looks like here

It looks like a series in a finding aid.

> **Series 9.** Provenance: found in collection. Extent: 1.4 linear at last inventory, 1.7 at the
> inventory before certification. Arrangement: [blank]. Condition: sound throughout. Access: no
> restriction; retrieval available on production of a description.

It has an extent and a condition and no origin. It is not described as anything. It has never done
anything. It arrived by the only two routes anything arrives by, and both of them are procedural.
**A new deity in this series is a growing count in a provenance class, and it never gets a
paragraph.** If a drafter finds themselves writing a paragraph about one, the piece has already
failed law seven.

## CUSTODY, AND WHY THIS SERIES ONLY HAS TWO CLASSES

`error-doctrine.md` gives four custody classes for a wrong number and rules: *"A wrong number ships
only if you can name who is holding it. Name the custodian, or delete the error."* Applied here it
produces a real, checkable constraint, and it is worth stating because a drafter will otherwise
reach for the parent's strongest instrument and find it unavailable.

- **Class 1, held by the narrator**, is **unavailable.** The narrator here is impeccable by law one.
  It cannot state a discrepancy in its own arithmetic because it does not have any.
- **Class 2, held by a named person in the world**, is **unavailable.** There is no clerk. Nobody is
  on the archive's side. The parent's strongest class runs on a man with a post and a chair who is
  quietly protecting a number, and this series has deliberately removed him.
- **Class 3, held by the form**, is the series' native class and carries almost everything: the blank
  cell in the intervals schedule, the blank arrangement statement, the disposition field that does
  not print, the greyed provenance. *"The absence has dimensions, so a reader can measure it."*
- **Class 4, held by the reader alone**, is rationed to one per season and must be the season's
  ending.

**The season's class 4, stated here because a working bible is already a spoiler index and this may
never appear in any published doctrine:** the header of every piece has read `returned complete`
since piece one, the index has a perfect record, and the requester in piece ten is the requester
from piece three, whose question was answered completely, on time, at no charge, and who still does
not know when her lease ends. **Nine complete returns and nobody got anything.** No line says it.
The reader checks it against a header they have been skimming for nine pieces.

## WHAT IS WALLED AND WHAT IS A DOOR

The parent's walls are the fourteenth's name, the correspondent's body, and whether the fire worked.
Five here, and each one says what dies if it is opened.

**Wall 1. What does the filing.** Never named, never ranked, never caused, never denied, never
addressed. *Opened:* it becomes an entity with a file. A thing with a file has a line on a list, and
a line on a list is a monster in a census, and this series' entire turn was that it has no line
because no form has a field for it. **Dies: the eldritch, permanently, and it cannot be recovered
by un-naming it later.**

**Wall 2. The repository's own extent, as at now.** The index may state its extent as at the last
inventory, and must, every time, with that qualifier. It may never state a current figure. *Opened:*
the index becomes correct about itself, law two falls, the narrator becomes trustworthy, and the
series is the parent with worse furniture. **Dies: the flip, which is the only reason this exists.**

**Wall 3. Whether the requesters are connected.** The request register is a holding and a reader
will want it to be a pattern. It is not one. Nine people, nine unrelated lives, one room. *Opened:*
coordination is a plan, a plan needs a planner, a planner is somebody who wants something, and the
anthology's founding rule is that nothing acts. **Dies: law seven, and with it the parent's engine,
in both series at once.**

**Wall 4. Where a residual class request goes.** It does not go anywhere. There is no room, no
shelf, no other side, no realm, no position in a line. *Opened:* the moment anything is measurable
about that queue, a length has been assigned to a blank cell by implication, and the parent's last
unspent cosmological card is spent from a sibling file. **Dies: the parent's charter, and the
parent's never list says exactly what goes with it.**

**Wall 5. Whether this repository is the parent's institution.** Never confirmed, never denied,
never gestured at, never made to nearly happen. The two works share a universe and not a cast, and
the join is a feeling a reader gets four times and cannot cash. *Opened:* the sibling becomes a spin
off with a continuity, "connected at a different level" collapses into "connected at the plot
level", and the parent acquires a canon it has to service. **Dies: both works' independence, and
this is the wall a drafter is likeliest to open by accident, with one helpful sentence, meaning
well.**

### The doors, and drafters should use all of them

Free, generative, and nothing is spent by opening them.

- Any designation, any closure date, any Standard value keyed to a world, any rebasing chain.
- Any office name, form number, revision number, service standard, retention rule, fee schedule,
  objection period, distribution list.
- Any requester's whole life, in their own words, in the slip. Every word of it.
- The physical condition of an item: folds, foxing, a repair, a water line, a hand that changes
  halfway down a page, a sheet cut from something larger.
- The **found in collection** count. It moves. It is the season's dread and it is a door, not a wall,
  because the items themselves are never explained.
- What a retrieval costs and who cannot pay it. Fees are the series' cruelty and they are all
  published.
- Referral. Which office declines to be the office that holds a thing. Referral loops are free, and
  they are the only place this series is allowed to be funny.

## WHAT THIS SERIES MAY NOT DO

Every one is checkable, and each exists because something specific breaks.

1. **The index is never wrong.** Not once, in any piece, about anything. Scope, never accuracy.
2. **No first person, anywhere on the archive's side.** No *I*, no *we*, no named clerk, no chair,
   no post held for twenty two galaxals by somebody who cared. That man is the parent's and he is
   the best thing in it and taking him is theft.
3. **Nobody is kept out.** There is no restriction, no clearance, no forbidden shelf, no locked
   room, no seal, no eyes only. There is no catalogue path, which is a different thing, and the
   difference is the series. A piece with a guard in it is not this series.
4. **No requester is a fool, a punchline, or wrong to ask.** Warmth, never contempt, is canon law and
   the satire register is what breaks it.
5. **No villain, no plan, no conspiracy, no secret, no entity that wants anything.** *"No villain,
   ever, in any piece."* Everything is published. Every fee is on a schedule. Every procedure can be
   shown to anybody who asks.
6. **No clean wins.** Nine complete returns and nobody gets what they came for, and the season does
   not soften one of them at the end.
7. **The residual queue is never located, measured, positioned or described.** See wall 4.
8. **The word dark never appears in a piece.** It is the author's title. In world, the state is
   **unlisted**, and unlisted is a state of the catalogue and not of the holding.
9. **Nothing is electronic.** Paper, a schedule, a fee, a stamp, a room, a trolley. No systems.
10. **Nothing is destroyed.** There is no procedure. A withdrawal is a record and the record of the
    withdrawal is a record.
11. **No found in collection item is ever explained.** Not by an office, not by a requester, not
    later, not partly.
12. **No character theorises.** Not about arrangement, not about scope, not about the residual class,
    not about why the count rises. Theorising is the reader's job and handing it to a character
    spends it.
13. **No em dashes or en dashes, anywhere, ever.** Comma, full stop, colon. The repo lints on it.
14. **No numbered ladder, sequence, tier, rank or pathway of ascent.** That is an import and a blind
    reader will name the book it came from.
15. **The series never explains its own joins to the parent.** Not in a piece, not in a blurb, not in
    a caption, not in a plate.

## THE JOIN

Four places, and a reader who has been through the parent will feel every one and be told nothing.

1. **Standing Order Six and the Concluded flag**, quoted verbatim in the registers that carry them.
   A parent reader knows what a rebasing did once, somewhere, to one line in a table, and knows that
   the restatement can be missed.
2. **The intervals schedule.** It is reproduced at most twice in the whole season, and it prints the
   unit names, the lengths, and **exactly one realm**, which is the blank one. The realm column is
   present. Six rows have lengths. A parent reader knows what the other realm names are and knows
   what a Standard time system is named after. This series never says.
3. **A word a form will not take.** Piece one is the parent's entire correction engine, run once, at
   a counter, for a sheet, with nobody named and no correspondent anywhere in it.
4. **A description that outlives the thing described.** The parent's keystone is a conversion nobody
   fixed that became the last surviving record of a world. This series is built out of that shape at
   room temperature and never once points at it.

**A fifth join is available and is refused permanently:** any confirmation that the two institutions
are the same institution. See wall 5.

## THE TELLER

Canon law five, carried across without amendment: *"If you write an entry and cannot name its
teller, the entry is not finished."*

**Here the teller is the requester**, and the slip is the telling. Nine of them across ten pieces.
They are the only people in the series, the only first person, the only handwriting, and the only
warmth. The heroes lose, as always, and here there are no heroes at all, only nine people who filled
in a form correctly.

**Two are difficult and they are picked here rather than at drafting time**, because a drafter under
deadline will pick the pieces that can most afford it instead of the ones that need it.

- **Hesk (5) is angry**, reasonably and specifically, and stays angry, and is right, and the piece
  does not resolve it or soften her at the end.
- **Vunn (7) will not say what he filed**, and the piece never finds out, and no document in the
  file reveals it. Every route to it is closed by a procedure that is correctly followed.

And a third, quieter: **Torgir (8) is not curious.** He reads the sentence the whole series is built
on, decides the figure is good enough for a budget, and files his search. He is completely correct.

## ART DOCTRINE

Short by design. The parent's four seasons must be identifiable as objects across a room, and this
one is a fifth object.

**A plate is a production tray on a reading room counter, photographed square on under the desk
lamp, after hours.** Season One is a survey form. Season Two is a warm hand measured page. Season
Three is that page burnt. Season Four is a wet notice pasted to a wall. This is **a tray of paper
that somebody will collect in the morning**, and it is the only one of the five where the object is
neither published, kept, destroyed nor executed, only produced.

**The chrome, byte identical on all ten, only the middle changes.** The tray, its edge, the lamp
fall from one side, the call number label clipped to the front, the fastener at the top left, and
the two rubber stamps: **RECEIVED** with a date, **CLOSED** with a date. Both on nine plates. **Plate
ten carries RECEIVED only**, and nothing about it is emphasised.

**Monochrome throughout, one paper stock, ten plates.** The only colour in the entire season is the
stamp ink, and it is the same violet on every plate. Colour identifies nothing. That is the
demotion, and it is one step further than the parent's Season Four, where colour at least still
identified a class.

**The hands.** `species.md`'s tether is that the corpus says hand or hands sixty three times and
*"a pair of hands doing careful work reads as a person no matter what it is attached to."* This
series has no hands in any frame, because the room is empty and the tray is photographed after
hours. **The tether is the handwriting.** The slip is the one object on every plate rendered at full
fidelity, ink and pressure and the crossing out and the hand getting smaller, and everything else in
the frame is flat. A person is present on every plate and no body is.

**Draw the mechanism, not a mood**, unchanged. A reader who has read the piece should be able to
point at the sheet in the tray that is the turn.

## WRITING A PIECE

1. **Start with the slip.** Somebody wants one specific thing for one specific reason. If you cannot
   write their reason in a sentence in their own voice, there is no piece.
2. **Work out what the index actually holds** that matches their description, and hold yourself to
   law three: the answer may not reach past what they were able to describe.
3. **Produce it completely.** Do not withhold. Do not delay. Do not have an office be difficult. The
   return is complete, correct and inside the standard, and the fee is fair, and they pay it.
4. **Find the gap.** What they wanted and what they asked for are not the same object, and the
   distance between the two is the entire piece.
5. **Count the documents.** At least two, at most five, at least one by a person and one by an
   office.
6. **Check the retrieval note against the register table** in this file, which is the authority on
   every number.
7. **Then read it back and cut every sentence where the index sounds like it has noticed
   something.** That is the only edit this series needs and it is the one that takes the longest.
